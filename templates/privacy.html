This policy explains what personal data {{ site_name }} collects, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It covers the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the Bulgarian Law on Personal Data Protection (Закон за защита на личните данни).

1. Who is responsible

{{ site_name }} ("we", "us") is the data controller for the personal data described here. We operate from Bulgaria. For any privacy question or request, contact us at {{ contact_email }}.

2. What we collect

  • Files you upload. The file content and its name and size, stored so it can be downloaded through its private link.
  • Transfer metadata. A random link token, upload time, chosen expiry, a download counter, and an anonymous capability key used so you can delete a file without an account.
  • Account data (optional). If you register, a username, an email address, and a securely hashed password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Technical data. Basic server/connection data needed to deliver the pages and protect the service, plus a session cookie (see section 6).

We do not sell personal data, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To provide the service — storing, delivering and expiring transfers. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep accounts working — authentication and your dashboard. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the service safe and available — abuse prevention and security. Legal basis: our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To comply with the law — e.g. responding to a lawful request. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

4. How long we keep it

  • Files are deleted automatically when the transfer expires, or sooner if you delete them. "Never" links stay until you remove them.
  • Anonymous transfer records are removed together with their file.
  • Account data is kept while your account is active. Ask us to delete your account and we will remove the associated personal data, subject to any legal retention duties.

5. Who we share it with

We do not share your files with third parties. We use a limited number of infrastructure providers (hosting, storage) strictly to run the service; they process data on our instructions. Where a provider is outside the EEA, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards under Chapter V GDPR.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We keep cookies to a minimum and use no advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

  • Session cookie — needed for security (a CSRF token) and, if you sign in, to keep you signed in. It is strictly necessary, so it does not require consent.
  • Preference storage — small local values (for example, remembering a chosen expiry) that never leave your browser.

Under the Bulgarian Law on Electronic Communications and the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC), strictly necessary cookies may be set without consent. Because we set no other cookies, no consent banner is required. Should that change, we will ask first.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and request data portability. See our GDPR page for how to exercise these rights and how to complain to the Bulgarian supervisory authority.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.
