{{ site_name }} operates from Bulgaria, so your personal data is protected by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679) together with Bulgaria's Law on Personal Data Protection (Закон за защита на личните данни, ЗЗЛД). This page summarises your rights and how to use them.

Who supervises us

In Bulgaria, the GDPR is enforced by the independent Commission for Personal Data Protection (Комисия за защита на личните данни — CPDP). You can contact the CPDP directly at any time:

  • Address: 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., 1592 Sofia, Bulgaria
  • Phone: +359 2 915 35 18 · Fax: +359 2 915 35 25
  • Email: kzld@cpdp.bg
  • Website: www.cpdp.bg

Your rights under the GDPR

  • Access (Art. 15). Ask whether we process your data and get a copy.
  • Rectification (Art. 16). Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Erasure (Art. 17). Ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten") — for example, deleting a transfer or your account.
  • Restriction (Art. 18). Ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Portability (Art. 20). Receive the data you gave us in a portable format.
  • Object (Art. 21). Object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent (Art. 7). Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Not be profiled (Art. 22). We do not make automated decisions that significantly affect you.

How to exercise your rights

Email {{ contact_email }} with what you would like to do. We will respond within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests), and exercising your rights is free. We may need to verify your identity — especially for anonymous transfers, where the private link token is the only thing that ties a file to you.

What we process and why

We keep processing to a minimum. In short: files and their metadata to run the service (contract), optional account details to provide your dashboard (contract), and basic security/technical data to protect the service (legitimate interests). We do not sell data or run advertising profiles. Full detail is in our Privacy & Cookie Policy.

International transfers

We aim to keep data within the EEA. If a service provider processes data outside the EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards under Chapter V of the GDPR (such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses).

Children

Under Bulgarian law the age at which a child can consent to information-society services is 14. We do not knowingly allow children under 14 to create an account or share personal data.

How to complain

If you believe we have handled your data unlawfully, please contact us first at {{ contact_email }} so we can put it right. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CPDP (details above) or with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.
