Makrotik Share exists for one reason: sending a file should not require an account, a subscription, or handing over your life story. Pick a file, get a private link, move on.

What we do

We run a lightweight file-sharing service built in Bulgaria and available to anyone. An upload gets an unguessable private link, streams straight to disk (up to {{ max_upload_human }} per transfer), and is removed automatically when the transfer expires. Signing up is entirely optional and only adds a personal dashboard for tracking and deleting the files you send.

How we think

  • Anonymous by default. No account is needed to upload or download — not for you, not for the person receiving the link.
  • The link is the key. The only way to reach a file is its random token. Keep it private and the file stays private.
  • Less is more. We collect the minimum needed to run the service and keep nothing longer than necessary. See our Privacy Policy for the details.
  • You stay in control. Set an expiry when you upload, and delete a file at any time from your dashboard or its link.

Made in Bulgaria
