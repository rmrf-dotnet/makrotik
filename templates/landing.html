How it works

Three steps. No sign-up.

1. Pick a file

Drag a file in or browse your device. Nothing leaves until you press upload.

2. Set an expiry

Pick how long the link stays alive — from an hour up to never. Expired files clean themselves up.

3. Share the link

Send the private link to anyone. They download it — no account needed on their side either.
